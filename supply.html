Coffee supply

Coffee supplyfor Singapore businesses.

Altiva is a coffee supplier based in Singapore, sourcing from coffee farms in Colombia. We bring the coffee here and handle the wholesale supply for the businesses that serve it — whether that calls for a specialty lot with a story behind it or a dependable commercial bean that has to taste the same every week.

Specialty coffee

Colombian specialty coffee beans for cafés and hotels that build a menu around the cup. Caturra Amarillo in natural and honey, semi-washed Catimor and F8, and Geisha, each with its own character.

Commercial coffee

Colombian commercial coffee beans for operations that run on volume and consistency — restaurants, hotel banqueting, offices and workplaces. Not a lesser coffee: a different job, and one that has to repeat without surprises.

Green or roasted

Supplied green from Colombia, or roasted in Singapore through our roasting partner so it reaches your bar close to the roast date instead of after months in transit.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you pour

    Volume, the market you serve and the bean you run today. Two working days to a reply, in your time zone.

  2. 02

Common questions

What buyers askbefore the first order.

Where does Altiva’s coffee come from?

From coffee farms in Colombia. Altiva selects the coffee at origin and brings it to Singapore, so every lot can be traced back to the ground it grew on.

Do you supply specialty coffee or commercial coffee?

Both. Specialty lots for businesses that want a distinctive cup and a story to tell, and commercial Colombian coffee for operations that need consistency and volume. Different coffee solutions for different business needs.

Who does Altiva supply in Singapore?

Cafés and coffee shops, restaurants and hotels, and offices and workplaces across Singapore. Altiva supplies both whole beans and the wholesale volumes those operations run on.

Is the coffee roasted in Singapore?

Yes. Coffee is roasted in Singapore by our roasting partner, so it reaches your bar days after roasting rather than months after shipping. Green coffee is also supplied directly from Colombia for partners who roast in house.

Can we try the coffee before we order?

Yes. Tell us the volume you handle and the market you serve, and we will send samples for your own cupping table before any commitment.

How do we start working with Altiva?

Send an enquiry through the form on our contact page or message the sales line. We reply to sample requests within two working days.
